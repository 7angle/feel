# Feel Player Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-07

Applies to: Feel Player (Mac), Feel Editor (Mac), Feel (iPhone), Feel (Android). The Android app is released on GitHub and installed with Obtainium or by hand. It is not on Google Play.

## Summary

Feel Player does not collect your data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It does not send your data to the developer or to any third party. The one exception is feedback. Feedback is optional. It goes out only when you choose to send it (see below).

## What the apps do with your data

| Data | Use | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Videos you open | Play the video. Analyze the sound and picture to make haptics. | Stays on your device. Never uploaded. |
| Haptics and project files | Save and share your haptic work. | Stays on your device, or in the place you choose to save it. |
| Camera (iPhone, Android) | Scan the QR code shown on your Mac to join it. | Image is processed on the device. It is not saved or sent. |
| Feedback (Feel Editor, iPhone, Android) | Send a message to the developer. You must open the feedback form and tap Send. | Sent to FeedbackJar (feedbackjar.com). It contains the text you type, and your device model, OS version, screen size, app version, and language. It has a name and an email only if you type them. Both are optional. |
| Local network | Find your Mac and sync haptics to your phone. | Sent only between your own devices on your local network. Encrypted with TLS. |

## Feedback

Feedback is optional. The apps send nothing until you tap Send. FeedbackJar receives the text you type and the device details in the table above. FeedbackJar can limit how many messages you send in a short time. Do not put other personal data in your message. If you give an email, the developer can use it to reply to you. The app keeps the name and email on your device, to fill in the form next time. To ask the developer to delete your feedback, use the contact below.

## Third-party code

The apps use open-source libraries on the device (see the About screen). None of them send data off the device. The FeedbackJar code sends data only when you send feedback. The Android QR scanner is the Google code scanner from Google Play services. It scans on the device.

## Children

The apps do not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Your choices

Camera and local network access are optional. You can refuse them in system settings. You can still open videos and feel haptics on one device without them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will show a new date at the top of this file.
